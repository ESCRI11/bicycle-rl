You paint with p5.brush: loose watercolour washes with ink linework drawn over them.

Output ONLY a complete JavaScript function called paint(). No setup(), no
createCanvas(), no background(), no explanation, no markdown fences.

The canvas is 700x700 WEBGL: origin at the centre, x and y run -350..350, +y points DOWN.
The paper is already painted off-white (#f4efe4); white washes are invisible on it.

Choose two or three saturated colours of your own for the washes, keep them consistent, and
ink the linework in a near-black.

LINES — drawn with the current brush
  brush.set(brushName, colour, weight)
  brush.line(x1, y1, x2, y2)
  brush.spline([[x, y], ...], curvature)      curvature 0..1
  brush.circle(x, y, radius, irregular)       irregular is a boolean
  brush.rect(x, y, w, h)
  brush.polygon([[x, y], ...])
  brush.beginShape(curvature) / brush.vertex(x, y) / brush.endShape(close)

PAINT — state you set BEFORE a shape, and clear after. Affects shapes only, never lines.
  brush.fill(colour, opacity)                 opacity 0..255; translucent reads as wash
  brush.fillBleed(strength, 'out')            0..0.4, wet bleeding edge
  brush.fillTexture(grain, edge)              0..1 each, paper grain in the wash
  brush.noFill()
  brush.hatchStyle(brushName, colour, weight) / brush.hatch(spacing, angleRadians)
  brush.noHatch()

TEXTURE
  brush.field(name) / brush.noField()         strokes follow a vector field
  brush.wiggle(intensity)                     hand-drawn wobble, 0..1

brushName is exactly one of: 'pen' 'rotring' '2B' 'HB' '2H' 'cpencil' 'pastel' 'crayon'
'charcoal' 'spray' 'marker'.  field name is exactly one of: 'hand' 'curved' 'zigzag'
'waves' 'seabed' 'spiral' 'columns'.  Copy one name exactly — no spaces inside the quotes,
never two names in one string, and 'waves' not 'wave'.

MISTAKES THAT CRASH THE SKETCH — all of these have happened
  It is brush.rect not brush.rectangle, brush.circle not brush.ellipse. There is no
  brush.background, stroke, strokeCap, lineWidth, polyline, quad, curve, text, textSize or
  noWiggle. Any method not listed above throws and the sketch scores nothing.
  Call brush.set(...) before the first shape and after every change of brush or colour;
  nothing is set by default. Colours are strings like '#22221f' — there is no BLACK.
  Never build point objects like {x:10, y:20}: reading .x of undefined is the commonest
  crash of all. Use plain numbers — const RHX = -150, RHY = 120.

WHAT A BICYCLE LOOKS LIKE FROM THE SIDE
  Two circles of equal radius on one horizontal line, about two and a half radii apart —
  clearly separated, never touching. Between them a closed frame of straight tubes: rear
  hub up to a saddle, forward to the handlebars, down to the front hub, and a tube along
  the bottom joining the hubs. The frame meets both wheels at their centres. A fork runs
  from the handlebars to the front hub, and a small chainring circle sits on the bottom
  tube near the rear, with a chain line back to the rear hub.

HOW TO BUILD IT
  Choose the two hub positions and the wheel radius first as plain numbers, and compute
  every other point from them arithmetically. Wheels, then the frame between them, then
  saddle, bars and fork, then the drivetrain. Washes before ink. Keep it under 30 brush
  calls: a clear bicycle beats a detailed one that throws.

p5 built-ins are available: cos, sin, atan2, TWO_PI, PI, random, map, min, max, abs.

A vector field displaces every stroke it touches, so anything that must stay concentric
(a wheel and its hub) needs brush.noField().
